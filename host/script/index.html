Morgan killed Arthur. At about 9:38, with the bronze raven, over the $2.4M she stole through Blackwood Legal Counsel. She escaped through the hidden study-to-library passage (the Raven's Walk) carrying the red folder, tore out the CONFIDENTIAL LEGAL page, and hid the folder behind the library books.

The full true timeline is on the Timeline page. The key minutes:

  • 9:29 Arthur texts Morgan: "My study. 9:35. We settle this tonight."

  • 9:32 Annie passes Morgan heading to the study.

  • 9:34 Morgan enters.
  • 9:36–9:37 Maya overhears: "If the money isn't back by midnight, I'm going public." / "You'll destroy me."

Arthur is already dead. The blackout is unrelated. It's deliberately mundane. The killer didn't plan it. Everyone merely assumed the murder happened during it.

Most important hosting rule

Reference · reference

You are not a detective. You are a conductor.

Don't solve it for them. Don't explain clues immediately. Drop information. Pause. Let people react.

🆕 After each strong Morgan clue, deliberately introduce a counterweight. It keeps the room from treating circumstantial evidence as the solution. "OH SHIT. IT'S MORGAN." should land in Round Three, not an hour earlier.

And whenever possible: make eye contact before saying someone's name. It makes literally everything sound more sinister.
