Carlotta "Carley" Blackwood

core Carley · confirmed · content/characters/carlotta.yaml

Preview as guest: Round 0 Round 1 Round 2 Round 3

Host notes.

Core (her Round Three note leads into the Kush and Lindsey financial reveal). False suspect A. Clara clears the will theory in Round Three. The note is a prop that must be in her Round Three envelope.

Secret

You're broke, you came home hoping your father would help, and he told you he was giving everything away. You have no alibi for the murder.

Mission

Find out who believes they're in Arthur's will. Collect names and report to Reggie.

Evening

  • 8:10 PM front-hall: Arthur greets you, kisses your cheek, and says: "At midnight, I'm giving it all away." Then he walks off to his study.
  • 8:15 PM–9:25 PM drawing-room: Drinking quickly. Avoiding Daniel. Being charming to people you despise.
  • 9:25 PM–9:45 PM conservatory: Alone among the orchids, smoking and furious, maybe crying. Nobody saw you. You have no alibi and you know it.

Round One

Tell the room what your father said to you at 8:10 in the Front Hall: **"At midnight, I'm giving it all away."** Yes, it means you were about to lose everything. Yes, that looks like a motive. Make them prove it.

Reveal:

  • Arthur told you at 8:10: "At midnight, I'm giving it all away."

Round Two

A destroyed will may have turned up. Everyone will look at you. You didn't do it, and you don't know who did. If Arthur's calendar surfaces showing **11:30 Carlotta**, admit you didn't know about any meeting. Admit, if pressed, that you were alone in the conservatory from 9:25 to 9:45.

Reveal:

  • You were alone in the conservatory from 9:25 to 9:45.

Round Three

This envelope contains a note in your father's handwriting. It was meant for your 11:30 meeting. Read it aloud: > **Carlotta — you were right. Tonight I stop protecting the people who > betrayed my trust. Whatever happens, know that none of this was your > burden to carry. — Dad** Then ask the room, and mean it: **"Who betrayed him?"**

Reveal:

  • Read Arthur's note aloud and ask "Who betrayed him?"
