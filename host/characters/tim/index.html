Host notes.

Core. Round One: strange library door. Round Two: the Tim to Joji cue. Round Three: blackout origin (supports Reggie's reveal). Rescue: "Timothy, you've been wandering around this house all evening. Anything unusual?"

Secret

You sell candid photos to The Keyhole, and tonight you have two that could ruin people: Clara leaving the study, and Maddie in the trust's necklace. You noticed the library door hours before anyone cared.

Mission

Find someone who knows something about the strange library door. Report who and what to Reggie.

Evening

  • 8:30 PM–8:55 PM front-hall: Shooting the hall and the gallery. At 8:44 you catch Clara coming out of the study, glowing, lipstick freshly applied. Click.
  • 9:00 PM–9:10 PM library: In the library for the bookcases. One bookcase doesn't sit flush with the wall, and there's a cold draft from behind it. Louis is in there tapping the paneling.
  • 9:10 PM–9:30 PM billiard-room: Photographing Jimmy Friedman's card game. Very cinematic, very little honesty.
  • 9:30 PM–9:45 PM dining-room: Setting up the midnight shot at the Supper table and helping Alma with place cards until she leaves around 9:39.
  • 9:45 PM dining-room: The dining-room chandelier dies first. Then the whole house. A crash from the kitchens below. You go toward the noise in the dark.

Round One

Reggie may ask whether you noticed anything unusual. You did: at about 9:00, in the library, **one bookcase didn't sit flush against the wall and there was a cold draft behind it.** Like a door, almost. You may also mention that Louis was in there tapping the walls.

Reveal:

  • You noticed a strange library bookcase/door with a cold draft at 9:00.

Round Two

Find Professor Joji Beaumont and ask him directly, in front of people: **"Professor, could that library door be some kind of passage?"** Then let him perform. Look amazed. You were right all along.

Reveal:

  • Ask Joji publicly whether the library door could be a passage.

Round Three

When the blackout comes up: you were in the dining room. **The dining-room chandelier went first, then the house**, and you heard a crash from the kitchens downstairs. It felt like it started below stairs, not anywhere near the study. Optional, if paid or if it's funny: produce your "photo" of Clara.

Reveal:

  • The blackout started in the dining room and kitchens, with a crash from downstairs.
