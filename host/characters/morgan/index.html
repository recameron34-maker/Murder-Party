Morgan Leibold

core Morgan · confirmed · content/characters/morgan.yaml

Preview as guest: Round 0 Round 1 Round 2 Round 3

Host notes.

The killer. Her page is deliberately the same shape and length as everyone else's (the tests enforce it). Optional prop: a folded "CONFIDENTIAL LEGAL" page for a final "empty your pockets" moment. Her Round Two envelope must physically contain the email prop.

Secret

You killed Arthur Blackwood at about 9:38 with the bronze raven to stop him exposing the $2.4 million you stole. You escaped through the hidden passage into the library, and the torn-out legal page is still on you.

Mission

Get at least two people to publicly accuse someone other than you. Tell Reggie when you've done it.

Evening

  • 8:00 PM–9:31 PM drawing-room: Charming everyone. At 9:29 Arthur texts: "My study. 9:35. We settle this tonight."
  • 9:32 PM east-corridor: You pass Annie coming back from the study with an empty tray. You smile. She smiles.
  • 9:34 PM–9:38 PM study: "If the money isn't back by midnight, I'm going public." You say, "You'll destroy me." He turns away. The raven is right there. You strike once.
  • 9:39 PM–9:41 PM library: With his red folder, through the panel by the fireplace and Silas's passage into the library. You keep the CONFIDENTIAL LEGAL page and hide the folder behind the books.
  • 9:41 PM library-corridor: You nearly walk into Alma. "Looking for the restroom," you say. She walks you there herself.
  • 9:42 PM–9:47 PM drawing-room: A glass in your hand before the lights go out. When they return you look as shocked as anyone.

Round One

Be helpful. Be appalled. Remind people of Kevin's threat, Molly's fake and Carlotta's inheritance. At the end of the round Reggie will read Arthur's last text, sent to you. Stay calm: a **scheduled meeting**, legal work.

Reveal:

  • When Arthur's phone is read out, admit the 9:35 meeting was real and was about "legal work."

Round Two

If the calendar shows **9:35 M.L.**, admit it's you. Then play your best card: read aloud Arthur's email (prop in this envelope): > *Morgan, I need you to review the Wellington, Monroe and Vale matters > before midnight. There are too many loose ends. — Arthur* *"I am literally his lawyer. Half the suspicious things you've found involve me because Arthur paid me to deal with suspicious things."*

Reveal:

  • Read Arthur's email aloud (prop in this envelope).

Round Three

They'll come for you: Annie in the corridor, **BLACKWOOD LEGAL COUNSEL**, **ML-0427**, and **LEGAL. $2.4M. MIDNIGHT.** in Arthur's hand. *"Of course my office appears on his notes. I'm his attorney. I've spent the last week investigating this with him."* Partially true, which is the best kind of lie. When Reggie asks who *you* believe did it, accuse someone.

Reveal:

  • When ML-0427 comes out, admit it is a Blackwood Legal Counsel login (but "half the office uses it").
