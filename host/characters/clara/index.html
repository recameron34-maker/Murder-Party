Host notes.

Supporting. Part Two's second study visitor (9:33). Round Three clears the Carlotta inheritance theory. If Clara isn't cast, Reggie can read the will quote as "something Arthur told Clara" from the host script.

Secret

You were Arthur's secret lover again, you know he tore up the will himself, and you walked toward the study at 9:33 and saw someone there you couldn't identify.

Mission

Get Carlotta alone and ask her what Arthur said about midnight. Report her answer to Reggie.

Evening

  • 8:30 PM–8:44 PM study: With Arthur, door locked. He tells you about the will. You leave at 8:44, fixing your lipstick.
  • 8:45 PM–9:32 PM drawing-room: Pretending to be cool and unbothered while glowing slightly.
  • 9:33 PM east-corridor: You walk toward the study, then see someone farther down the hall near the study door. You can't tell who. You turn back.
  • 9:34 PM–9:47 PM drawing-room: Back at the party with a fresh drink, annoyed at yourself. The lights go out at 9:45.

Round One

Admit you met Arthur in the study at 8:30, a private conversation between old friends. He seemed happy. Lighter. You're on better terms than people think. Do not elaborate. Let them imagine.

Reveal:

  • You met Arthur privately in the study at 8:30.

Round Two

Confess your second visit: **at 9:33 you walked toward Arthur's study to finish your conversation, but you saw someone farther down the corridor and changed your mind.** You couldn't see who. Yes, that puts you near the study in the critical window. Yes, you're a woman. Let them wonder.

Reveal:

  • You approached the study at 9:33, saw someone farther down the hall, and turned back.

Round Three

Now clear Carlotta, even if she doesn't deserve it. Tell the room what Arthur told you at 8:30: **"I tore up the old will myself. Everyone keeps assuming tonight is about inheritance. It isn't."** [[if:savanah]] And if Savanah is still calling herself the widow, you may inform the room, icily, that you were *there* when Arthur sobered up in 2019. [[/if]]

Reveal:

  • Arthur told you he tore up the will himself: "tonight isn't about inheritance."
