Secret

You suspect, though you've never proven it, that a passage connects Arthur's study to the library. Arthur forbade you to investigate the study wall. Also, you stole a servants' bell.

Mission

Find someone willing to help you investigate the architecture. Recruit an assistant, give them a task, report the partnership to Reggie.

Round One

When the talk turns to how a killer could get in or out, or when Reggie asks whether the house has secrets, tell everyone: **Blackwood Manor was built with servants' passages inside its walls.** Silas didn't like seeing his staff. Most were sealed. *Most.* Keep the study and library to yourself for now.

Reveal:

  • The house has hidden servants' passages, some possibly unsealed.

Round Two

Reggie will give you an old floor plan (prop). Study it. It marks the **STUDY** and the **LIBRARY**, with a faint line between them. When Tim asks whether the strange library door could be a passage, perform the realization: **the passage connects Arthur's study directly to the library.** Someone could enter the study, leave through the wall, and come out among the books, never using the study door.

Reveal:

  • The hidden passage connects the study to the library.

Round Three

If asked where the passage ends: **behind a bookcase in the library**, opened from that side by pressing the carved raven on the third shelf. From the study it's a panel by the fireplace. If anyone asks about the legendary "Raven Room": you now believe the Raven Room *is* the passage, a narrow cold corridor with a bench, a candle niche and Silas's initials in the brick. The family called it the Raven's Walk.

Reveal:

  • The passage ends behind a bookcase in the library.
