Host notes.

Noor is pregnant in real life (and in character). Annie's non-alcoholic "Reckoning" is canon, so make sure there's a good mocktail. Keep her role seated and spoken; she shouldn't have to hunt for evidence.

Core. Time of death (9:35 to 9:40) in Round Two is THE first major twist. Ross's rescue: "Dr. Beaumont. You've had more time to examine what happened. What can you tell us?"

Secret

You've known since 9:47 that Arthur died before the lights went out, between 9:35 and 9:40. You've kept it to yourself because you wanted to be certain, and because a doctor saying "murder" in this house is how doctors get uninvited. You also prescribed Arthur's "vitality" pills.

Mission

Privately ask three people when they last saw Arthur alive. Write down their answers. Bring the list to Reggie.

Evening

  • 8:00 PM–9:15 PM drawing-room: Trapped by Kushington explaining forensic accounting. You diagnosed him with "enthusiasm" and escaped.
  • 9:15 PM–9:45 PM drawing-room: By the fire with Joji and Hannah, the whole time. Joji was describing load-bearing walls. You were counting the minutes until supper.
  • 9:47 PM study: When the lights come back and Reggie finds Arthur, you go in with him. One blow to the head. The body is already cooling. That bothers you a great deal.

Round One

Shea is going to say something chilling about Arthur's health. When Reggie turns to you, confirm that Arthur had a serious heart condition, but that **his illness did not cause his death.** He was struck. This is officially murder.

Reveal:

  • Arthur's illness did not kill him. He was struck on the head.

Round Two

Now the big one. When asked (or when the moment feels right), say it plainly: **Arthur died between 9:35 and 9:40 PM.** The lights went out at 9:45. He was dead *before* the blackout. Everyone has been investigating the wrong minute.

Reveal:

  • Arthur's time of death was between 9:35 and 9:40 PM, before the blackout.

Round Three

One more medical detail if anyone asks: it was a **single blow**, struck close. No defensive wounds, no struggle. Arthur let his killer get near him. He wasn't afraid of whoever it was. Support the timeline: 9:31 alive (Annie), 9:35 to 9:40 dead (you), 9:45 blackout.

Reveal:

  • It was a single blow from close range with no struggle; Arthur wasn't afraid of the killer.
