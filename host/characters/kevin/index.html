Secret

You threatened Arthur at 9:20 and you live entirely on his money, which stops at midnight. But you were on the terrace, on camera, during the murder.

Mission

Get someone to believe your argument with Arthur wasn't about money. (It was.) Report the believer to Reggie.

Evening

Round One

People heard you at 9:20. Admit it before they do: **you told Arthur, "You're going to regret this."** You meant he'd regret destroying the family. Of course you did. A cufflink may turn up near the study. **It's yours.** Recognize it out loud and go pale. You have no idea when you lost it.

Reveal:

  • You told Arthur "You're going to regret this" at 9:20.
  • The cufflink found near the study is yours.

Round Two

You've remembered two things: 1. **The cufflink**: you were moving furniture into the study with Reggie this afternoon, sleeves rolled up. Ask Reggie to confirm it. 2. **The terrace has a security camera.** You were out there from 9:22 until the lights came back. Demand that Reggie check the footage. Reggie may make you sweat first. Sweat.

Reveal:

  • You lost the cufflink helping Reggie move furniture this afternoon.
  • Ask Reggie to check the terrace camera.

Round Three

You finally check the pockets of the coat you grabbed at 9:22, Arthur's coat. There's a **folded bank statement** inside (prop in this envelope). Don't read it out. When Reggie calls for final evidence, **hand it to him.** It's his to read.

Reveal:

  • Hand Arthur's folded bank statement (from his coat) to Reggie.
