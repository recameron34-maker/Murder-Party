James "Jimmy" Calloway

core Jimmy · confirmed · content/characters/jimmy.yaml

Preview as guest: Round 0 Round 1 Round 2 Round 3

Host notes.

Core. The Morgan library sighting at 9:40 (Round Two) is solution-critical. The "why was James cut from the will" scandal resolves in Round Three. Don't confuse him with Jimmy Friedman; Reggie calls this one "James."

Secret

You saw Morgan near the library at 9:40, you owe Jimmy Friedman $300,000, and you've been sleeping with Alma, the archivist.

Mission

Get three people to agree that Kevin had the strongest inheritance motive. Report the three names to Reggie.

Evening

  • 8:50 PM–8:58 PM study: You confront Arthur about the will. He doesn't raise his voice. He says, "Tonight isn't about you, James." It's worse than shouting.
  • 9:00 PM–9:38 PM billiard-room: Losing at cards to Jimmy Friedman, which feels on-brand. Daniel and Martin are playing too.
  • 9:38 PM front-hall: You leave the game to call your lawyer about contesting the will. The only phone signal in the house is at the mouth of the Library Corridor.
  • 9:40 PM library-corridor: On the phone, pacing, you see Morgan inside the library, near the doorway, smoothing her dress. Odd place for the family lawyer. You turn away to finish your call.
  • 9:41 PM–9:47 PM front-hall: Still arguing with your lawyer's voicemail when the lights go out.

Round One

Admit it before someone else does: **Arthur removed you from his will.** You confronted him about it at 8:50. Yes, you were angry. No, you didn't kill him. You were losing at cards from 9:00. Then pivot hard to Kevin. *Kevin* expected the whole house.

Reveal:

  • Arthur removed you from his will, and you confronted him at 8:50.

Round Two

Now say it: **around 9:40 you left the card game to make a phone call by the Library Corridor, and you saw Morgan in the library.** You thought nothing of it then. You're thinking about it now.

Reveal:

  • You saw Morgan near/in the library at about 9:40.

Round Three

Someone will ask why Arthur cut you out. Tell the truth, with as much dignity as you can manage: you borrowed **$300,000 from Jimmy Friedman** against your inheritance, and Arthur would not have his estate "used as collateral by sharks." It's humiliating. It isn't murder. [[if:savanah]] And if Savanah is still calling herself the widow, you were best man at that wedding, and you were there when Reggie and the lawyers flew out two weeks later with the annulment. Say so, kindly. [[/if]]

Reveal:

  • Arthur cut you out because you borrowed $300,000 against your inheritance from Jimmy Friedman.
