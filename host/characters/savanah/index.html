Host notes.

Real-life ties (Ross): Savanah knows Jimmy, Kim, Ross and Carley. Her character is built around them. "Jimmy" is assumed to be the player of James Calloway; if Ross meant Jimmy Friedman, move the best-man tie there. Reggie is in on her backstory: play along when she calls you "nephew."

Flex (maybe RSVP), theatrical. Carries zero solution-critical clues. Her red herring ("the widow inherits") is resolved by Kim's 2019 Keyhole clipping (Round Three) and by Clara. Whereabouts: the drawing-room piano, far from the study.

Fallback if not cast.

Nothing depends on Savanah. If she isn't cast, the widow red herring never appears. Clara's and Kim's Savanah lines are inside [[if:savanah]] blocks and disappear automatically.

Secret

Your marriage to Arthur was annulled in 2019. You know it. You're bluffing about being his widow because you're broke.

Mission

Get two different people to call you "Mrs. Blackwood" without being told to. Report them to Reggie.

Evening

  • 8:00 PM–9:00 PM drawing-room: Introducing yourself as "the widow, darling, well, the wife, as of an hour ago."
  • 9:00 PM–9:45 PM drawing-room: At the piano, singing a medley of the songs Arthur requested in Vegas. Everyone in the room watched you the whole time; you made sure of it.

Round One

Arthur is dead and you're the grieving widow. Weep magnificently. Then mention, delicately, that "as his wife" you suppose the estate is now… a matter for you. The room will now think you had the best motive of anyone. Enjoy it. You have the best alibi too.

Reveal:

  • Announce you're Arthur's widow from a 2019 Las Vegas wedding.

Round Two

People will dig. Defend the marriage with documents (your "wedding photo") and passion. If anyone mentions Morgan, insist "that woman made me sign things while I was unwell."

Reveal:

  • Accuse Arthur's lawyer of making you sign papers in 2019.

Round Three

If the annulment comes out, concede with enormous dignity. Then point out that a woman who was *singing at a piano in front of twenty people* from 9:00 to 9:45 didn't kill anyone.

Reveal:

  • Concede the annulment if proven, and stand on your piano alibi.

Connections

  • Arthur Blackwood: Your husband of a fortnight, in 2019. He was sweet, drunk and sad. You liked him more than you meant to.
  • James "Jimmy" Calloway: James Calloway was Arthur's minder that Vegas weekend and his best man at 3 a.m. in a borrowed bow tie. The only person here who actually saw the wedding. You've been texting him all week to "get your stories straight."
  • Carlotta "Carley" Blackwood: Arthur's daughter, which makes her, as you keep reminding her, your stepdaughter. Carley has asked you to stop. You will not stop.
  • Kim: An old friend from the party circuit, and the only one here who calls you "Mrs. B" to your face. Kim always seems to know which of your stories are true.
  • Reggie (Ross): Reggie flew out in 2019, paid your hotel bill and helped make the whole thing quietly disappear. You call him "nephew." He hasn't stopped you.
  • Clara: The ex-fiancée whose exit sent Arthur to Vegas. She looks at you like something on her shoe. Mutual.
  • Morgan Leibold: The lawyer who brought the papers in 2019. If anyone can contradict you, it's her. Watch her.
  • Lady Courtney if courtney: You recognize a performer when you see one. "Lady" Courtney is no lady. Takes one to know one.
