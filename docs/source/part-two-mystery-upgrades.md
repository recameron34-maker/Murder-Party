# MURDER AT BLACKWOOD MANOR

### Mystery Upgrades

## 1. THE FOUR FALSE THEORIES

Players should spend most of the night bouncing between these possibilities.

## SUSPECT A: CARLOTTA BLACKWOOD

#### The inheritance theory

Carlotta is Arthur's estranged daughter.

Arthur told her:

**"At midnight, I'm giving it all away."**

Hannah confirms Arthur was preparing an enormous charitable transfer.

Kevin has been removed as a possible heir.

Arthur's will is mysteriously missing from his study.

Then players find something worse.

### PHYSICAL CLUE: THE DESTROYED WILL

Hidden somewhere in the apartment is a partially burned/torn legal document.

Visible text:

**LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF ARTHUR BLACKWOOD**

and:

**"...the remainder of my estate shall pass..."**

The recipient's name has been destroyed.

Someone tried to eliminate the will.

#### Why Carlotta looks guilty

She's Arthur's daughter.

She has been estranged from him.

Arthur said he was giving everything away.

She was angry about how Arthur handled the family fortune.

The will is destroyed.

And Arthur is dead before his midnight announcement.

That's a legitimate murder theory.

#### THE TRUTH

Carlotta didn't destroy it.

Arthur did.

In Round Three, Clara reveals Arthur told her:

"I tore up the old will myself. Everyone keeps assuming tonight is about inheritance. It isn't."

This clears the strongest version of the Carlotta theory.

## SUSPECT B: MOLLY MONROE

#### The fraud theory

Molly sold Arthur a $750,000 counterfeit painting.

Martin confirms it's fake.

Arthur knew.

Then players find:

### PHYSICAL CLUE: ARTHUR'S THREATENING LETTER

Hide inside/behind a picture frame:

**Molly —**

**We need to discuss the Monroe acquisition tonight.**

**I know what happened.**

**If you knowingly deceived me, I will have no choice but to involve the authorities.**

**— A.B.**

That looks AWFUL.

#### Additional evidence

The bronze raven murder weapon belongs to Arthur's art collection.

Molly knew exactly where it was.

She visited Arthur's study earlier.

She had an enormous financial motive.

#### THE TRUTH

The letter says:

**"IF you knowingly deceived me."**

Later, another note establishes Arthur had concluded Molly was also deceived by the forger.

She isn't completely cleared until Round Three.

## SUSPECT C: KEVIN BLACKWOOD

#### The angry heir theory

Kevin expected he might inherit Blackwood Manor.

Arthur tells him:

**You're getting nothing.**

They have a loud argument at 9:20.

Multiple people hear Kevin threaten:

"You're going to regret this."

Important:

Add that line to Kevin's Round One envelope.

Kevin must admit he said it.

He meant Arthur would regret destroying the family relationship.

But obviously it sounds murderous.

### PHYSICAL CLUE: KEVIN'S CUFFLINK

Leave a men's cufflink near the study/murder area.

Kevin recognizes it as his.

#### THE TRUTH

Kevin lost it earlier that afternoon while helping Reggie move furniture.

Ross knows this.

But Ross shouldn't reveal that immediately.

Let Kevin panic.

Eventually Kevin's terrace-camera alibi proves he was outside during the murder.

## SUSPECT D: LINDSEY VALE

#### The money theory

This one is especially important because it prevents:

**Missing money = Morgan.**

Lindsey is Arthur's private banker.

She knew about the suspicious transfers before almost anyone.

She has access to financial information.

She's initially reluctant to reveal details because of confidentiality.

And she's dating the investigative journalist/podcaster Arthur was secretly feeding information to.

That's suspicious.

### PHYSICAL CLUE: BANK NOTE

Players discover a note from Arthur:

**L.V.**

**We need to discuss the transfers before midnight.**

**Come prepared to explain everything.**

This appears to implicate Lindsey Vale.

#### THE TRUTH

Arthur wasn't accusing Lindsey.

He was asking Lindsey to explain the banking evidence.

She was helping him investigate.

But players shouldn't learn that until Round Three.

## 2. ONE VERY IMPORTANT CHANGE TO MORGAN'S EVIDENCE

Currently, Arthur's final bank statement says:

**MORGAN. MIDNIGHT. DONE.**

That's basically a confession written by the victim.

Remove it.

Too easy.

Instead Arthur writes:

**LEGAL. $2.4M. MIDNIGHT.**

Now players know the money relates to Blackwood Legal.

But that doesn't necessarily mean Morgan stole it.

Morgan can reasonably argue:

"Of course my office appears on his notes. I'm his attorney. I've spent the last week investigating this with him."

And here's the fun part:

**That's partially true.**

Arthur DID ask Morgan questions about the account.

Morgan can therefore defend herself without sounding ridiculous.

## 3. GIVE MORGAN A PIECE OF EVIDENCE THAT ACTUALLY HELPS HER

This is important.

In Round Two, Morgan should reveal something herself.

She produces a copy of an email Arthur sent her:

**Morgan,**

**I need you to review the Wellington, Monroe and Vale matters before midnight. There are too many loose ends.**

**— Arthur**

This establishes that Morgan's legal work genuinely involved multiple scandals.

Suddenly:

Being on Arthur's calendar isn't weird.

Being near his study isn't necessarily weird.

Being involved in the financial investigation isn't necessarily weird.

Arthur's "LEGAL" note isn't necessarily incriminating.

Morgan can truthfully say:

"I am literally his lawyer. Half the suspicious things you've found involve me because Arthur paid me to deal with suspicious things."

That's a strong defense.

## 4. THE BLACKMAIL CASH SYSTEM

This could be one of the most fun parts.

Print fake:

## BLACKWOOD BLACKMAIL NOTES

Give every player **$3,000** at the beginning:

- $1,000
- $1,000
- $1,000

Or use poker chips / fake bills.

Ross explains:

"Arthur believed information was the only currency more powerful than money."

"Unfortunately, you're all about to prove him right."

## HOW BLACKMAIL WORKS

A player may offer another player **$1,000 in Blackmail Cash** and ask:

**ONE question about their character, whereabouts, secret or knowledge.**

If the other player accepts the money:

#### THEY MUST ANSWER TRUTHFULLY.

They do NOT have to give their entire secret.

They only have to truthfully answer the specific question.

Example:

**"Were you in Arthur's study tonight?"**

If the answer is yes, they must say yes.

But they don't have to volunteer why.

## THE MURDERER EXCEPTION

Morgan is the **ONLY person allowed to lie after accepting Blackmail Cash.**

Her murderer card explicitly says:

#### YOU MAY ACCEPT BLACKMAIL CASH AND LIE.

This is incredibly fun because at the end Ross can reveal:

"There was one person in this house whose information could be bought..."

"...but never trusted."

## REFUSING BLACKMAIL

Anyone may refuse an offer.

But refusal looks suspicious.

If you refuse, you don't get the money.

Players can raise the offer:

$1,000.

$2,000.

$3,000.

Still don't want to answer?

Fine.

Now everyone REALLY wants to know why.

## EARNING MORE MONEY

This adds a fun incentive.

Ross can award $1,000 Blackmail Notes for:

- Excellent accusation
- Finding physical evidence
- Completing a secret objective
- Particularly committed performance

He should announce:

"Excellent detective work. Arthur would have hated it. One thousand dollars."

## OPTIONAL RULE: BUY FROM REGGIE

Players can also pay Ross/Reggie:

#### $3,000 = PRIVATE HINT

Ross draws from a stack of hint cards.

Examples:

**TIME:**
"The blackout may be distracting you."

**MONEY:**
"Ask who first noticed the transfers."

**HOUSE:**
"The library matters more than it appears."

**MOTIVE:**
"Not every financial scandal is connected."

These should nudge, never solve.

#### JIMMY FRIEDMAN

#### LEXI

## 6. PHYSICAL EVIDENCE HUNT

Don't dump all the clues out at once.

Ross introduces them progressively.

Players can search the **designated game areas** of the apartment.

Important rule:

"Nothing is hidden inside personal belongings, bedrooms, drawers or cabinets. If it isn't obviously part of the game area, leave it alone."

Use envelopes stamped:

## BLACKWOOD EVIDENCE

## PHYSICAL CLUE 1 — THE CUFFLINK

#### Available: Round One

Leave near your fake "study."

It belongs to Kevin.

Makes Kevin look extremely suspicious.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 2 — THE THREATENING LETTER TO MOLLY

#### Available: Round One

Hide behind a framed picture.

Points strongly toward Molly.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 3 — THE DESTROYED WILL

#### Available: Late Round One

Tear/burn-looking edges safely with scissors/tea staining rather than actually burning it during the party.

Hide near a fireplace, trash basket or decorative box.

Points toward Carlotta/Kevin/inheritance.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 4 — ARTHUR'S CALENDAR

#### Available: Round Two

Print:

**ARTHUR BLACKWOOD — SATURDAY**

8:30 — Clara
8:50 — James
9:20 — Kevin
9:31 — Drink
9:35 — M.L.
10:00 — Lindsey
11:30 — Carlotta
12:00 — Announcement

This is GOOD because:

**M.L. could mean Morgan Leibold.**

But it could also potentially be interpreted as something else initially.

Morgan admits it's probably her.

She says:

"Yes. I had a meeting scheduled. I'm his attorney."

This doesn't prove murder.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 5 — THE "L.V." NOTE

#### Available: Round Two

**L.V.**

**We need to discuss the transfers before midnight.**

**Come prepared to explain everything.**

This shifts suspicion hard toward Lindsey.

Let it breathe.

Do NOT immediately clear her.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 6 — THE LIBRARY MAP

#### Available: Round Two

Give Joji an old-looking floor plan.

Mark:

**STUDY**

**LIBRARY**

and a faint line between them.

Don't label it "SECRET PASSAGE."

Joji gets to figure it out.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 7 — THE TORN FINANCIAL LEDGER

#### Available: Late Round Two

Numbers:

**$300,000**
**$450,000**
**$275,000**
**$600,000**
**$375,000**
**$400,000**

Total:

**$2,400,000**

Beside the transfers:

**AUTHORIZED: BLC**

What does BLC mean?

Blackwood Legal Counsel?

Blackwood Luxury Corporation?

Blackwood Lending Company?

Let people argue.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 8 — THE BLACK THREAD

#### Available: Round Two

A piece of black/dark fabric caught on the raven.

This should NOT match Morgan uniquely.

Ideally several guests are wearing black.

Carlotta probably is.

Clara might be.

Shea might be.

Morgan might be.

This creates chaos without lying.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 9 — THE DRAFT LETTER

#### Available: Round Three

Arthur's unfinished letter:

**I trusted you because you were supposed to protect this family. Instead, you used that trust to—**

The rest is missing.

This sounds like:

Carlotta?

Kevin?

Morgan?

Lindsey?

Someone protecting the family professionally?

Keep it ambiguous.

## PHYSICAL CLUE 10 — THE FINAL BANK DOCUMENT

#### FINAL CLUE

This is the evidence that finally tightens the case.

Show:

**BLACKWOOD HOLDINGS**

Transfer authorizations totaling:

**$2,400,000**

Authorization origin:

**BLACKWOOD LEGAL COUNSEL**

Authorized user ID:

**ML-0427**

Now it becomes much harder for Morgan to explain away.

But even THIS doesn't prove murder by itself.

Players still need:

Annie → Morgan heading toward study.

Maya → argument with woman.

Noor → 9:35–9:40 death.

Joji → passage.

Jimmy + Alma → Morgan near library afterward.

Financial documents → motive.

That's what makes the solution satisfying.

## 7. ADD ONE MORE BIG RED HERRING: THE MISSING FOLDER

After the murder, Ross announces:

Arthur's desk has an empty space where Maddie remembers seeing a **red leather folder** earlier.

The folder is missing.

Everyone assumes the killer took it.

During Round Two, allow players to search.

Eventually they find:

## THE RED FOLDER

Hidden somewhere suspicious.

Inside:

- Molly's painting authentication
- Kevin's inheritance documents
- Lindsey's banking memo
- Blackwood charity paperwork
- A page labeled **CONFIDENTIAL — LEGAL**

But the legal page has been torn out.

This is juicy because Arthur was clearly assembling everyone's scandals before midnight.

Was he planning to expose ALL of them?

Was someone trying to protect themselves?

Morgan actually DID remove the key financial page.

But because the folder contains dirt on multiple guests, almost anyone could have wanted it.

## 8. GIVE SOMEONE ELSE A SECRET VISIT TO THE STUDY

Right now Morgan being the only person near Arthur around the murder is too neat.

Change that.

#### CLARA:

At **9:33**, Clara approached Arthur's study because she wanted to finish their earlier conversation.

Before reaching the door, she saw someone farther down the hall and changed her mind.

She cannot identify them.

#### SHEA:

At approximately **9:40**, Shea also walked toward the study because Arthur had asked her to bring him medication later that evening.

Before she reached it, she heard people elsewhere and returned to the party.

Now multiple women have legitimate reasons to be in the area.

Maya's:

**"A woman said you'll destroy me."**

becomes much less immediately useful.

## 9. ROSS SHOULD ACTIVELY CONTROL SUSPICION

Ross should NOT simply dump clues.

After each strong Morgan clue, deliberately introduce a counterweight.

#### EXAMPLE

Annie:

**"I saw Morgan walking toward the study."**

Ross:

"Which is certainly interesting."

"Although according to Arthur's calendar, Morgan was supposed to meet him."

"So unless attending a scheduled meeting is now murder..."

*pause*

"...we need more."

Alma reveals Morgan near library.

Ross:

"Also interesting."

"But Professor Beaumont has just informed us that half this house appears to contain doors nobody knew existed."

"Being in a library is not yet a capital offense."

Kush reveals Blackwood Legal Counsel.

Morgan:

"I'm his lawyer. Of course my office authorized legal transfers."

Ross:

"She's annoyingly correct."

"The question isn't whether Morgan had access."

"The question is whether she abused it."

This prevents the room from treating circumstantial evidence as the solution.

## 10. IDEAL SUSPICION CURVE

#### END ROUND ONE

Strong suspects should include:

**Kevin** — disinherited + threat + cufflink.

**Molly** — $750K fraud + threatening letter + murder weapon knowledge.

**Carlotta** — estranged daughter + giving fortune away + destroyed will.

**Lindsey** — mysterious financial activity.

**Morgan** — 9:35 meeting.

Maybe **Louis** — literally snooping around hidden walls.

Nobody should stand out enormously.

#### MIDDLE ROUND TWO

Now serious theories become:

**Lindsey** stole $2.4M.

**Morgan** stole $2.4M.

**Carlotta** killed Arthur over inheritance.

**Molly** killed Arthur to hide fraud.

**Kevin** killed Arthur after being disinherited.

And:

**Maybe the missing money and murder aren't even connected.**

That's important.

#### END ROUND TWO

Kevin becomes mostly cleared.

Molly's motive weakens.

Carlotta's inheritance theory starts collapsing.

Now:

**Morgan + Lindsey** should be the strongest financial suspects.

But Shea/Clara remain possible as the unidentified woman.

#### ROUND THREE

Lindsey's role flips:

She wasn't hiding the theft.

**She discovered it.**

Carlotta is cleared of the inheritance motive.

The passage timeline becomes clear.

Only then do:

Morgan's meeting

- Morgan heading toward study
- female argument
- Morgan near library
- hidden passage
- legal credentials
- stolen money
- Arthur's scheduled message

lock together.

That's the moment people should realize:

## OH SHIT. IT'S MORGAN.

Not an hour earlier.

After Morgan is exposed, Ross asks:

"Morgan..."

"Did anyone pay you for information tonight?"

Let people react.

Then:

"There was one rule Arthur neglected to mention."

"Everyone in Blackwood Manor was bound by Blackmail Cash..."

"...except the murderer."

"Morgan was the only person tonight permitted to take your money..."

"...look you directly in the eye..."

"...and lie."

This should cause absolute outrage.

Perfect ending.
