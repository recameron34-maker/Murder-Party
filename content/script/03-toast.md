Once most people have drinks:

"Before my uncle inevitably makes this evening about himself…"

*Pause.*

"…a toast."

*Raise glass.*

"Blackwood Manor has the pleasure of celebrating TWO birthdays this weekend. Alexandra St. Clair and Maya Rose."

"Arthur apparently prepared surprises."

*Look around suspiciously.*

"Knowing my uncle, one will be sentimental and the other will involve paperwork."

"To Lexi and Maya."

*Toast.*
