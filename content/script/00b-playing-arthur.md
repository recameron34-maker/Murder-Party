🆕 From the first guest until the birthday cake, **you are Douglas
Cameron**: seventy-one, dying (only Noor and Shea know), and about to
set everything right at midnight. Roderick, his much younger half-brother,
is "late from the village".

**The look.** Tan suit, black silk tie, the flat cap he wears indoors
because it's his house, silver hair (a wig). The tie matters: it's the
black thread found on the raven in Round Two, and Shea says so in Round
Three. Wear it all evening so people remember it. Shoes that also work
with Roderick's burgundy suit.

**Leave early.** In the solution Douglas dies seven minutes before the
blackout, so nobody may see him alive in the last stretch before the lights
go. You leave at the cake, about fifteen minutes before.

**How he plays.** Courteous, amused, unhurried, and cold underneath. He
collects people and lets them know it. He never raises his voice except
once, at Kevin. Every question about money, the will, the announcement or
anybody's secret gets the same answer: **"At midnight."**

**What Douglas knows and must never say before he dies:**
- Who stole the money, how much, or that anyone did. (He knows it's Morgan.
  He treats her exactly like everyone else: one dry line, nothing sharper
  than what Clara, Lindsey or Molly get.)
- That he's dying.
- That he hired Kushington, that Lindsey found the theft, that Malik has a
  message scheduled for midnight, or that Albie's séance is his idea.
- Savanah's annulment, Lexi's paternity, Molly's forgery, Maddie's
  necklace, Courtney's act, anyone's affair.
- Anything about walls, passages, or the library.

**The real couples** (Noor and Joji, Lindsey and Malik, Maya and Kevin):
no jokes about either one straying. Douglas can be cruel to Kevin about
money, never about Maya.

**Your lines with each guest** are in **Arrivals**. The ones marked
*quietly* are the exact words on their cards; say them and walk away.

**When you leave.** At the cake you say the family rule and go to your
study, change into Roderick in the closet, and fire the blackout at about
8:30 (**The birthday cake**, then **The blackout**). Nobody else acts
anything out; the guests only have to solve it.
