"The lights went out at 9:45."

*Pause.*

"So Douglas may have been dead BEFORE the blackout."

This is the first major twist.

**After Noor's reveal.** *Look at everyone.* "We've been investigating what happened in the dark." "But perhaps…" "…nothing happened in the dark."

Let them process.
