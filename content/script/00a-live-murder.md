---
title: The live murder (for Ross's eyes only)
round: 0
kind: reference
---
🆕 Tonight the murder really happens, offstage, while the party is round
the cake. You play Arthur until Morgan kills him in the study. She escapes
through the hidden door, exactly as in the story; you change into Reggie in
the study closet and follow her through it, then walk in through the front
door, late from the flooded road, and find your own body.

**Before the night**

1. **Ask Morgan's player, privately, whether she wants to do it.** If she
   says no, nothing else changes: you go to the study alone, change, and
   leave by the same door.
2. **Test the shelf twice.** Open the hidden door from the study side on
   your own, quietly; step through; slide the shelf back onto its tape marks
   from the landing side. Clear the heavy books off the bottom shelves first.
3. **Rehearse the change** with a timer. Under four minutes.
4. **Walk Morgan through it once, before guests arrive**: the study, the
   raven prop, the door, the shelf, the tape marks, the front door. Do it in
   person. Never put it on her page or in a text: her phone page has to look
   like everyone else's, and she gets no cue text at the cake.
5. **Late guests**: tell them to text you from the street. Nobody comes up
   the stairs alone between the cake and the blackout.

**On the night** (minutes after you leave the cake)

- **0:00** In the study. Rope down, lamp on, the red folder open on the desk
  and the raven prop beside it.
- **+1** Annie brings the Reckoning. "Last one, Annie." She goes.
- **+2** Morgan comes in. Argue low; the cake is loud. Then, clearly:
  - You: **"If the money isn't back by midnight, I'm going public."**
  - Morgan: **"You'll destroy me."**
  - Turn your back. She lifts the raven; you go down behind the desk. No
    contact, no scream. She sets the raven down beside you.
- **+4** Morgan goes through the hidden door, slides the shelf back onto its
  marks, walks in through the front door and back to the cake. If anyone
  asks where she's been: "Looking for the restroom." (It's her line in the
  story.)
- **+4 to +8** Change in the closet. Leave the body behind the desk: tan
  jacket, black tie, wig, and the flat cap on the floor by the raven. The
  folder and the tan trousers go in the bag. Burgundy suit, overcoat,
  umbrella, a spray of "rain" on your hair and shoulders.
- **+9** Listen at the hidden door, go through, and put the shelf back on its
  marks. Stand by the front door, well back from the top of the stairs.
- **+10** Fire **Blackwood blackout**. When the lights come back low, open the
  front door. You're Reggie, and you're late.

**What to tell Morgan** (in person, before guests arrive):

> "You did it, and tonight you actually do it. When the cake comes out I go
> to my study. When Annie goes off with my drink, count to sixty and follow.
> You'll pass her coming back: smile. In the study we argue quietly. Then I
> say 'If the money isn't back by midnight, I'm going public,' and you say
> 'You'll destroy me.' I turn my back. Lift the raven, I drop, you put it
> down beside me. Never touch me with it. Then out through the bookshelf
> door, shelf back on the tape, in through the front door and straight back
> to the cake. If anyone asks, you were looking for the restroom. When I
> walk in as Reggie, be as shocked as everyone. Tell nobody, even after the
> reveal, until I do."

**If something goes wrong**

- **Someone wanders into the study** before Morgan strikes: Arthur throws
  them out. "Out. I'm busy." Morgan waits in the Family Room until they've
  gone back to the cake.
- **The shelf sticks**: Morgan walks back the ordinary way, through the
  Family Room and the Living Room. Nobody at the cake can see the study
  end, and the restroom line still works. You do the same, but in the dark:
  fire the blackout from the study, cross to the front door by phone torch
  in the fifteen seconds, step onto the landing and come in when the lights
  return.
- **Someone is on the landing**: wait in the study and use the same
  dark crossing.
- **Morgan's player says no**: you die alone. Same change, same door.

**Afterwards**: at the very end (After the reveal), tell everyone it really
happened.
