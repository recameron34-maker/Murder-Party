🆕 **You're on the landing as Reggie**, front door shut, the shelf back on
its marks (see **The live murder**). Tap **Blackwood blackout** in the Alexa
app. The dining room dies first, then the kitchens, then the house; thunder;
15 seconds later the lights come back low, with the study glowing red.
(Lights → Setup has the routine step by step.) Stand still in the dark,
well back from the stairs. **Do NOT leave lights out for 90 real seconds.**

When the lights come back, open the front door. Wet coat, umbrella, out of
breath.

"Sorry. Sorry! Arthur sent me to the village for champagne at seven, and the lower road had other ideas."

*Shake the umbrella.* "Why is everyone standing in the dark?"

*Let them tell you.* "Well. That seems ominous."

"Where's my uncle?"

*Someone will say the study.* "Of course he is. He doesn't come out for cake." "I'll fetch him."

*Walk to the study. Noor may follow you in; her card says she does. Take a breath. Come back. Do not smile.*

"Ladies and gentlemen. Arthur Blackwood is dead."

*Pause.*

"He is lying behind his desk."

"Beside him is the Blackwood Raven, an eight-pound bronze statue from his collection."

"It appears to have been used to strike him."

🆕 "And there is an empty space on his desk. Madeleine, I believe you were in the study earlier. Was there something there?"

🆕 *Let Maddie describe the red leather folder.* "A red leather folder. It's gone. Whoever did this took it with them."

"The storm has washed out the lower road. I came up it twenty minutes ago; it isn't there any more. The police are on their way. But until they arrive…"

*Look around.*

"…nobody leaves Blackwood Manor."

🆕 If anyone looks at you: "Before anyone asks, I've spent the evening in a ditch on the lower road. The tow-truck driver can vouch, and he charged me double." If anyone says you look just like Arthur: "Everyone says that. He hated it."
