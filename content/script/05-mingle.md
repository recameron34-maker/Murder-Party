Give them 15 to 20 minutes. They know their characters (they read them
before the party) but DO NOT have Round One envelopes yet. 🆕 No phones
tonight: everything they need is in their heads and, later, in the
envelopes.

🆕 **Douglas works the room.** Don't sit. Drift from group to group, listen
more than you talk, and leave every conversation a little worse than you
found it. Everyone wants something from you tonight; the answer to all of it
is *"At midnight."* The meetings on their cards (Clara at half past eight,
Molly, James, Maddie and Kevin in the study) are backstory: don't stage them.

If things are slow, make them introduce themselves:

"Perhaps everyone should tell me why they think they're important enough to be in my house tonight."

Go around quickly. Douglas is unimpressed by everyone, equally.

🆕 Quietly hide the Round One physical evidence now if you haven't (cufflink
near the study, Molly's letter behind a frame). Hold the destroyed will for
late Round One.
