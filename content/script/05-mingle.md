Give them 20 minutes. They know their characters but DO NOT have Round
One envelopes yet. 🆕 Their phones already show their "history" texts, so
encourage them to read their phones.

🆕 **Arthur works the room.** Don't sit. Drift from group to group, listen
more than you talk, and leave every conversation a little worse than you
found it. Everyone wants something from you tonight; the answer to all of it
is *"At midnight."* The meetings on their cards (Clara at half past eight,
Molly, James, Maddie in the study) are backstory: don't stage them.

If things are slow, make them introduce themselves:

"Perhaps everyone should tell me why they think they're important enough to be in my house tonight."

Go around quickly. Arthur is unimpressed by everyone, equally.

🆕 **Optional: the row with Kevin, live.** Only if Kevin's player is up for
it (ask before the party). Send the text below; he comes to find you. In
front of whoever is near, flatly: **"You're getting nothing."** He shouts
the line on his card, **"You're going to regret this!"**, takes the dark
overcoat off the coat rack and storms off toward the terrace. He stays out
there (or in the back hall, if it's cold) until the lights come back, then
hangs the coat back on the rack. Hannah, Maddie and Shea already "heard"
this on their cards; now everyone has.

🆕 Quietly hide the Round One physical evidence now if you haven't (cufflink
near the study, Molly's letter behind a frame). Hold the destroyed will for
late Round One. Leave Arthur's Reckoning (a Sazerac, or a whisky) poured at
the bar for Annie.
